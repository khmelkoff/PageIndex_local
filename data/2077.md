## Abstract

Science fiction and video games have long served as valuable tools for envisioning and inspiring future technological advancements. This position paper investigates the potential of Cyberpunk 2077, a popular science fiction video game, to shed light on the future of technology, particularly in the areas of artificial intelligence, edge computing, augmented humans, and biotechnology. By analyzing the games portrayal of these technologies and their implications, we aim to understand the possibilities and challenges that lie ahead. We discuss key themes such as neural links and brain–computer interfaces, multimodal recording systems, virtual and simulated reality, digital representation of the physical world, augmented and AI-based home appliances, smart clothing, and autonomous vehicles. The paper highlights the importance of designing technologies that can coexist with existing preferences and systems, considering the uneven adoption of new technologies. Through this exploration, we emphasize the potential of science fiction and video games like Cyberpunk 2077 as tools for guiding future technological advancements and shaping public perception of emerging innovations.

## 1. Introduction
The rapid pace of technological advancements has led to an ever-increasing interest in understanding and predicting the future of technology. The capacity of science fiction to stimulate actual scientific exploration, technological advancement and their impact on society remains a topic of sustained fascination [1], [2], as seen in works such as Neuromancer [3], Snow Crash [4], Do Androids Dream of Electric Sheep? [5], Little Brother [6], Star Trek [7] and The Three-Body Problem [8]. Film serves as a compelling medium for illustrating the intricate relationship between science fiction and technological progress. These cinematic depictions enrich the ongoing scholarly discourse on the ethical and technological implications of emerging innovations, particularly in sectors like healthcare and aerospace [9]. More recently, video games have emerged as an interactive medium that offers unique opportunities to explore futuristic concepts and ideas, with some experts arguing that these ideas are inevitable [10]. One such video game, Cyberpunk 2077, has garnered attention for its portrayal of a technologically advanced society and the potential challenges and opportunities it presents. The games developers have detailed the artistic and technological aspects of creating its immersive cyberspace [11]. Its companion book, The World of Cyberpunk 2077 [12], offers an in-depth look into the games lore and setting and provides a comprehensive understanding of the games world.

Despite the rich narratives provided by science fiction literature and film, there is a significant gap in how these narratives connect with real-world technological foresight and societal implications. Current academic literature often focuses on speculative depictions of the future without sufficiently addressing how these visions intersect with contemporary technological trends and societal challenges. This paper addresses this gap by positioning Cyberpunk 2077 as a form of entertainment, but also a crucial cultural piece that bridges speculative fiction and technological foresight, offering a unique, interactive way to engage with emerging technologies and their potential impact.
Set in a dystopian future, Cyberpunk 2077 presents a detailed vision of an urban landscape dominated by powerful corporations that shape and govern the direction of technological advancements. This backdrop vividly intertwines social, political, and economic themes, providing a rich narrative for players to navigate. Cyberpunk 2077 has not only captured the imagination of gamers worldwide but has also sparked discussions among academics, researchers, and industry professionals about the likelihood and implications of such a future [13], [14], [15], [16].
The game presents a nuanced view of technology, exploring themes such as disability, gender, and the relationship between humans and machines [17], [18]. These themes raise significant ethical and social questions, illustrating the potential of video games as a medium for stimulating debate and influencing future advancements. As an illustration, Fox [15] delves into the portrayal of disability in Cyberpunk 2077 and the potential trajectory of cyborg healthcare. Notably, while the game offers detailed depictions of technologically advanced societies, history underscores that the uptake of innovations is often uneven. This disparity is shaped by factors like economic differences, cultural inclinations, and regulatory landscapes [19], [20], [21], a notion echoed profoundly in the game [12].
Cyberpunk 2077, as a significant work within the science fiction genre, presents a unique and immersive exploration of futuristic technologies and their societal implications. Unlike traditional science fiction narratives, Cyberpunk 2077 provides players with interactive, playable scenarios that dig into the ethical, social, and moral dilemmas posed by emerging technologies.
Due to this, Cyberpunk 2077 stands out within the science fiction landscape by providing an interactive, immersive experience that goes beyond traditional narratives. Unlike other media, the game allows players to actively engage with scenarios that reflect ethical, social, and moral dilemmas posed by advanced technologies. Themes such as disability, gender, and the complex relationship between humans and machines are explored in nuanced ways that raise significant ethical and social questions [17], [18]. These themes are reflective of ongoing technological debates and also provide a speculative, but also predictive lens through which we can consider the trajectory of technological innovations.
In our contribution, we examine the key technological themes and advancements presented in Cyberpunk 2077 and compare them with the current state of technology, aiming to move beyond a mere inventory of these technologies and themes by analyzing how they are integrated into the games narrative and specially world-building, providing unique insights that are both reflective and predictive of real-world technological advancements. By doing so, we identify existing challenges and opportunities for further development in various domains, including human augmentation, brain–computer interfaces, simulated reality, digital representation, smart homes and appliances, autonomous vehicles, and advanced smart assistants. Furthermore, we discuss the importance of designing technologies that can coexist with existing user preferences and technologies, ensuring a gradual and effective transition towards a more technologically advanced society [10], [15].
The novelty of our work lies in its approach to analyzing Cyberpunk 2077 as a foresight tool that critically examines the integration of advanced technologies within a complex social fabric. In this context, we aim to move the technological foresight frontier forward by systematically comparing the games depiction of technological advancements, such as human augmentation, brain–computer interfaces, and autonomous systems, with current state-of-the-art technologies and research. By contextualizing Cyberpunk 2077 within the broader landscape of science fiction, we highlight how the game aligns with or diverges from existing technological trends and predictions, offering insights that are both reflective of current advancements and predictive of future possibilities.

## 2. Literature review
In this section, we review existing literature on the role of science fiction in predicting and inspiring technological advancements, as well as studies on the specific technologies mentioned in the presentation. Our aim is to establish the context and show how the papers ideas fit into the broader academic conversation.

### 2.1. Intersection of science fiction and technological foresight
The intersection of science fiction and technological foresight has been widely explored in recent studies, revealing how speculative narratives can offer valuable insights into the governance, development, and public perception of emerging technologies. Science fiction, as a genre, not only reflects societal anxieties and aspirations but also serves as a tool for imagining alternative futures that challenge conventional thinking about technological progress.
Recent works have highlighted the role of creative writing techniques from science fiction and fantasy in enhancing technology foresight processes. Marshall et al. introduced the Story Thinking model, which applies skills from science fiction and fantasy writing to help transdisciplinary teams better inhabit possible futures, empathize with affected stakeholders, envision plausible scenarios, and engage inshared visions of technologys impact [22]. This model complements existing foresight approaches by enabling researchers to explore complex dimensions of large-scale technological projects in ways that traditional methods often overlook. Similarly, Kymalainen introduced science fiction prototyping as a method for discussing socio-technical issues within emerging technology research [23]. This approach allows researchers to experiment with speculative scenarios, using storytelling as a medium to anticipate the socio-ethical implications of technological innovations. By integrating narrative techniques into foresight practices, these methodologies enhance the capacity to explore diverse and often unforeseen outcomes of new technologies.
The theoretical grounding of futures and foresight studies has been a persistent challenge, as highlighted by Münch et al. who conducted a bibliometric review demonstrating the limited application of scientific theories within the field [24]. This lack of a robust theoretical base has hindered the recognition of foresight studies as a rigorous academic discipline. However, integrating theories from social sciences, such as critical theory, can enrich the understanding of how technologies shape and are shaped by societal forces. Freedman argued that science fiction is a privileged genre for critical theory, serving as a fertile ground for examining the socio-political and ethical dimensions of technological change [25]. By tracing the relationships between science fiction and critical theory, Freedman illustrated how speculative narratives can provide a nuanced critique of contemporary socio-technical systems. This perspective is essential for understanding the broader implications of emerging technologies and for developing foresight practices that are critically aware of underlying power dynamics.
Science fiction has a profound influence on public perceptions and expectations of technology, shaping both the development of new innovations and their acceptance by society. Wilsons study on the impact of science fiction futures depicted in popular culture found that these narratives directly affect how the public perceives and interacts with new technologies, particularly in fields like robotics and human–computer interaction [26]. By reflecting technological dreams and nightmares, science fiction helps frame the discourse around emerging technologies, influencing both public opinion and policy-making. Miller argued that science fiction-inspired approaches offer significant contributions to the governance of new technologies by enhancing the capacity to reflexively assess the social dynamics of socio-technical systems [27]. These approaches allow for a more nuanced understanding of how technological developments might unfold in different contexts, enabling better-informed decisions that consider both technical feasibility and societal impact.
Incorporating decolonial perspectives into foresight practices can provide a more just and ethical approach to technology development. Mohamed et al. explored the application of decolonial theory as sociotechnical foresight in artificial intelligence, proposing tactics to align AI research with principles of justice and equity [28]. This approach emphasizes the importance of considering diverse cultural and ethical perspectives when envisioning the future of technology, ensuring that technological advancements contribute to greater societal well-being rather than perpetuating existing inequalities. Schicks examination of speculative bioethics further highlights the need to critically assess visions of future technologies [29]. As speculative technologies increasingly shape present-day ethical discussions, there is a growing imperative to ground these debates in the realities of current technological capabilities and societal needs.
Design fiction has emerged as a powerful tool for engaging the public in discussions about the potential outcomes of new and emerging technologies. Heidingsfelder et al. developed a design fiction methodology that encourages public reflection on the ramifications of technological advances, bridging the gap between speculative futures and real-world concerns [30]. By creating narrative-driven, speculative artifacts, design fiction fosters critical engagement with possible technological trajectories, making the abstractions more tangible and the future more accessible.
However, while these studies explore various aspects of science fictions impact on foresight, they often lack an interactive component that engages users directly with the speculative technologies. This paper addresses this gap by examining Cyberpunk 2077 as an interactive medium that not only depicts futuristic technologies but also allows players to actively engage with the ethical and societal dilemmas these technologies present. By situating Cyberpunk 2077 within the context of science fiction and foresight, we can offer a novel perspective on how interactive narratives can enhance our understanding of technological futures.

### 2.2. Science fiction and technological advancements
Science fiction has a long history of providing a platform for speculating about future technological innovations and their impact on society. The genre often serves as a source of inspiration for researchers and technologists, with numerous real-life inventions tracing their origins to ideas first presented in science fiction stories [31], [32]. The influence of Willian Gibson is also notable. While his novel Neuromancer [3] did not presage the internet itself, which had its origins in the ARPANET of the 1960s and the adoption of TCP/IP in 1983, Gibsons work provided an influential vocabulary – cyberspace – and a vision of a world transformed by networked computing and advanced cyborg technologies. This vision strongly shaped popular discourse about the World Wide Web in the 1990s. Gibsons work also inspired VR researchers, as detailed in Benedikts Cyberspace: First Steps and Cavallaros Cyberpunk & Cyberculture: Science fiction and the work of William Gibson[33], [34]. The 1995 film Johnny Mnemonic, also based on William Gibsons story [35], played a significant role in popularizing virtual reality technologies, fictional depictions of VR are older, dating back to stories such as Vernor Vinges influential True Names. Moreover, other films slightly before Johnny Mnemonic helped shape public anticipation of VR. For a thorough history of VRs influence on cinema, see David Kirbys Lab Coats in Hollywood: Science and the Cinema (MIT Press, 2011) [36]. The 1966 television series Star Trek presciently introduced the concept of mobile communication devices, a vision that materialized with Motorolas launch of the inaugural flip phone, the StarTAC, in 1996 [37], [38], [39]. Similarly, the idea of a tablet computer can be traced back to Arthur C. Clarkes 2001: A Space Odyssey [40]. Stanley Kubricks seminal 1968 work, provided also an early examination of the ethical and computational complexities inherent in artificial intelligence, exemplified by the character HAL 9000 in the subsequent same named film [41]. The depiction of 3D holographic technology in George Lucass Star Wars [42] series has become iconic and inspired practical developments in the field. For a detailed documentation of the history and connection to holography, see Sean F. Johnstons Holographic Visions: A History of New Science (Oxford, 2006) [43].
Rajaniemis The Quantum Thief[44] explored the transactional nature of data privacy and access rights with the depiction of the gevulot, which echoes in the current suggested use of blockchain for the authentication of information [45], [46]. Furthermore, Blade Runner 2049 explores the technological feasibility and ethical considerations of 3D printing in the context of creating synthetic humans, a concept inching closer to reality due to advancements by firms such as Organovo [47].
Researchers have explored the symbiotic relationship between science fiction and technological development, arguing that the genre can serve as a catalyst for innovation by providing a vision of potential future technologies [48]. Moreover, science fiction has been used as a tool for technology forecasting, helping to identify emerging trends and assess their potential impact on society [49], [50]. Given its immersive and interactive nature, the cyberpunk genre – especially through mediums like video games – may represent the next frontier in predicting and actively inspiring technological futures, offering insights both ethical, social, and technological dilemmas.
Despite these valuable contributions, existing literature often lacks an in-depth examination of how interactive, immersive mediums like video games can serve as powerful tools for foresight and technological speculation. We analyze Cyberpunk 2077 as a unique interactive narrative that mirrors contemporary technological trends offering a dynamic platform to explore the societal implications of future technologies. The most popular media often lack an interactive component that engages users directly with speculative technologies. There are comparable works to Cyberpunk 2077 themes in TV-shows such as Black Mirror [51] and Westworld [52], movies such as The Matrix [53]. Even other videogames such as Deus Ex [54], and Detroit: Become Human [55], also explore themes of technology and society. However, although Black Mirror critiques individual technologies, often through standalone narratives that limit exploration to isolated scenarios, and both The Matrix and Westworld address AI and simulated realities, they rely on linear storytelling that restricts audience interaction and immersion. On contrast, Deus Ex and Detroit: Become Human share Cyberpunk 2077s focus on human augmentation and AI ethics, but their linear narratives, even when allowing the player to make multiple choices, limit user interaction to somehow predefined pathways. What sets Cyberpunk 2077 apart is its interactivity set in an open world, allowing players to directly experience and influence these speculative futures, and make their character live in different ways, making it a uniquely immersive foresight tool.

### 2.3. Video games as a medium for future technologies
As the video game industry has evolved, it has increasingly served as a platform for exploring futuristic concepts and ideas. Video games offer a unique opportunity to engage with potential future technologies in an interactive manner, enabling players to immerse themselves in the game world and experience the consequences of these technologies first-hand [56], [57]. Some recent studies have focused on the potential of video games for inspiring and predicting technological advancements, highlighting the role of game developers in creating plausible and engaging visions of the future [58], [59].

### 2.4. The unique contributions and predictive potential of Cyberpunk 2077
Cyberpunk 2077, while based on the earlier Cyberpunk tabletop RPG [60], offers a distinct and immersive exploration of futuristic technologies through its interactive gameplay and richly detailed world-building. Unlike static representations in books or films, the game allows players to experience the implications of these technologies first-hand, creating a unique and impactful narrative experience that can inspire and inform future technological advancements. The interactive nature of Cyberpunk 2077 provides players with a unique opportunity to engage with and reflect on complex technological and ethical issues. Cyberpunk 2077s detailed world-building sets it apart from other works in the genre. The games narrative intricately weaves together various technological themes within the socio-political landscape of Night City.
Cyberpunk 2077 has made a significant impact on popular culture, sparking widespread discussions about the future of technology and its societal implications. Despite its controversial release [61], the games themes of technological advancement, corporate power, and social inequality resonate with contemporary concerns. This cultural relevance makes the game a valuable lens through which to examine public perceptions and ethical considerations surrounding emerging technologies. The games depiction of a dystopian future serves as a cautionary tale, emphasizing the need for careful consideration of the societal impacts of technological progress.
While Cyberpunk 2077 draws on established themes from the cyberpunk genre, it offers a modern reinterpretation that incorporates contemporary technological trends and challenges. The game updates classic cyberpunk motifs – such as the fusion of human and machine, the omnipresence of corporate control, and the struggle for personal identity in a high-tech world – with new insights and scenarios that reflect the current state of technology. This blend of historical context and modern innovation provides fresh perspectives on familiar themes, making the game a relevant and insightful exploration of the future. Many of the technologies in Cyberpunk 2077 have been previously explored in other cyberpunk works, but the games interactive format and complex narrative structure provide a different medium for these explorations.
By comparing Cyberpunk 2077 to its predecessors and contemporaries, we can identify what makes its approach unique. For example, its use of real-time decision-making and the impact of player choices on the storyline allow for a deeper engagement with the ethical dimensions of technological advancement. Compared to other sci-fi works like Black Mirror, The Matrix, and Deus Ex, Cyberpunk 2077 offers a more intricate and participatory engagement with AI ethics, cybernetic augmentation, and dystopian futures. Black Mirror typically presents isolated cases of technological misuse, focusing on extreme hypothetical scenarios to highlight ethical dilemmas. While effective as a warning, its episodic nature means that it rarely examines the long-term systemic integration of AI or cybernetics into society. The Matrix, in contrast, deals with AI as a singular existential threat, portraying artificial intelligence as an antagonistic force that controls human existence rather than an embedded part of daily life. This perspective, while philosophically rich, does not explore the incremental and often ambiguous ways AI technologies shape modern societies Deus Ex, as an interactive game, incorporates themes of cybernetic augmentation but does so within a structured gameplay framework where augmentation is primarily treated as a functional game mechanic rather than a societal force. While it raises ethical questions about human enhancement, it presents augmentation as an individualistic choice rather than an inevitable consequence of technological and corporate expansion. Cyberpunk 2077, by contrast, embeds cybernetic augmentation into the fabric of its world, illustrating the economic, social, and political forces that drive and regulate human enhancement. The game challenges players to consider augmentation beyond personal utility, instead emphasizing its societal consequences—such as corporate exploitation, inequality, and systemic dependency on technology. In contrast, Cyberpunk 2077 offers a more nuanced examination of AI ethics. Rather than depicting AI as inherently benevolent or malevolent, the game presents artificial intelligence as a contested space of power struggles, corporate interests, and ethical ambiguity. AI entities in the game exist within a legal and economic framework, reflecting contemporary debates about AI governance, autonomy, and accountability, situating AI within a broader societal structure.
Cyberpunk 2077 allows open-ended exploration of corporate AI governance, bio-enhancement inequalities, and algorithmic bias through dynamic player interactions. By forcing players to confront the social consequences of these augmentations firsthand, rather than through passive observation, the game functions as an active foresight tool, immersing users in speculative futures they must navigate, rather than merely witness.
However, existing analyses often overlook the games potential as a foresight tool that actively engages players in speculative futures, rather than merely presenting them. We examining how Cyberpunk 2077 reflects and challenges current technological paradigms, offering a dynamic, interactive platform that bridges science fiction with real-world technological trends and societal implications.

## 3. Methodology
In constructing a solid theoretical foundation for our analysis, several established theories and frameworks have been identified as particularly relevant. Scenario Planning [72] is a foresight tool widely used in both corporate foresight and academic studies, allowing researchers to explore multiple future scenarios based on current trends . This approach facilitates understanding the potential implications of different trajectories, helping organizations and policymakers prepare for diverse possibilities. Actor-Network Theory (ANT), developed by Latour and Callon [73], provides a framework for examining the interconnectedness of technology, human actors, and institutions. ANT is particularly useful in understanding how technologies shape and are shaped by social structures, emphasizing the complex dynamics between human and non-human actors. Critical Future Studies [74] further contribute to this foundation by focusing on the ethical implications and power dynamics in future-oriented thinking. This framework critiques dominant narratives about the future, advocating for more inclusive and pluralistic approaches to foresight . Lastly, Cyborg Theory, with key contributions such as Haraways A Cyborg Manifesto, [75] explores the merging of human and machine, offering a critical perspective on technologys impact on human identity and the evolving boundaries between nature and technology .
Loosely based on these ideas, in this article, we aim to systematically analyze the key technological themes and advancements presented in Cyberpunk 2077 and compare them with the current state of technology. To achieve this, we followed a three-step methodology, which we outline below.

### 3.1. Data collection
First, we collected data from the Cyberpunk 2077 video game and its associated official materials, such as guides, trailers, and developer interviews. We focused on identifying the technological concepts and innovations present in the game, paying particular attention to the technologies that were emphasized in the presentation. This data served as our primary source for understanding the vision of the future as portrayed by the game developers.
The data collection included a detailed examination of the game content, focusing on the portrayal of various technologies and how they integrate into the games narrative and gameplay. As a starting point, we dedicated approximately 200 h to replayed the game with a special emphasis in immersing ourselves in the technologies, taking notes of some key aspects presented to the player.
For a more systematic data collection, we familiarized ourselves into a wide array of official guides and trailers. The guides provided in-depth insights into the games world, offering detailed descriptions of the technological elements and their functionality within the games universe. The trailers, on the other hand, served as a visual representation of these technologies, offering a glimpse into their potential real-world applications and implications.
A complementary source of our data collection was the analysis of developer and actor interviews. We reviewed a substantial corpus of over 10 interviews with the games developers, sourced from various media outlets and gaming conferences, publically available in Youtube in popular gaming channles such as GameSpot, IGN, GamingBible ore GamesRadar These interviews were instrumental in understanding the developers perspectives on the future technologies they envisioned and incorporated into the game. They provided valuable context regarding the rationale behind the inclusion of specific technologies and how they envisage these technologies evolving in the real world, and the actors approach towards the interaction with the world.

### 3.2. Thematic analysis
Next, we conducted a thematic analysis of the collected data to identify key themes and technological advancements within the game. We followed Braun and Clarkes six-phase approach to thematic analysis [76], [77], which involved:
- Familiarizing ourselves with the data by immersing ourselves in the game world and closely examining the identified technologies.
- Generating initial codes by identifying interesting features of the data and systematically coding these features.
- Searching for themes by collating the generated codes into potential themes.
- Reviewing themes by refining the identified themes and ensuring that they are supported by the data.
- Defining and naming themes by providing clear definitions and names for each theme.
- Producing the final report by presenting the findings of the analysis in a coherent manner.
This process allowed us to identify the key technological themes in Cyberpunk 2077 and organize them into coherent categories for further analysis.

### 3.3. Comparison with current technologies
In our final analytical phase, we compared the futuristic technological themes from Cyberpunk 2077 with the current state of technology, leveraging our expertise in AI and edge computing. Instead of a systematic review, we adopted a narrative style approach, using our expertise to select, interpret, and synthesize the relevant literature and recent technological developments. To conduct this expert-driven narrative review, we followed these steps:
Step 1. Keyword-Driven Literature Exploration: We chose relevant keywords to explore the existing literature. We used databases such as IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, PubMed, and ScienceDirect, employing a combination of keywords related to each identified technology theme. This search was complemented by the use of Google Scholar to ensure the inclusion of gray literature and the latest research outputs. This exploration was not limited to systematic database searches but extended to identifying seminal and cutting-edge papers indicative of current trends.
Step 2. Narrative Synthesis: We synthesized the findings from our literature exploration in a narrative format, highlighting how current developments in AI and edge computing align with or diverge from the technologies portrayed in Cyberpunk 2077.    
Step 3. Expert Insight and Forward-Looking Perspectives: We studied the selected literature and suggested future works to speculate on probable next developments, potential roadblocks, and emerging opportunities. This included a critical examination of the feasibility, ethical considerations, and potential timeframes for the realization of the technologies depicted in the game.
Step 4. Comparative Analysis: We conducted a comparative analysis of the identified themes against the backdrop of current technologies, assessing their realism, potential impact, and how they reflect or diverge from existing technological trajectories.

### 4.1. Overarching themes
In addition to the specific technologies and advancements, Cyberpunk 2077 presents several overarching themes that pervade its vision of the future. Reflecting on the broader trends and principles that shape the development and application of new technologies in the games world, these overarching themes provide a broader context for understanding the specific technologies and advancements present in Cyberpunk 2077, highlighting key principles and trends that shape the games portrayal of the future.
- Ubiquitous Sensing: (Everything Sensing) In the games future, virtually every device, object, and even human beings are integrated with a multitude of sensors. This constant sensing enables real-time data collection and analysis in a truly distributed manner, which in turn drives more informed decision-making and responsive technologies.
- Interconnectivity: (Everything Connected) The world of Cyberpunk 2077 is characterized by ubiquitous interconnectivity, with all devices, systems, and even humans constantly communicating and collaborating. This interconnectivity allows for the formation of very large-scale networks of sensors and data sources, enabling more efficient and coordinated responses to various challenges.
- Autonomy: (Everything Autonomous) Many technologies in the game are autonomous, meaning they can operate independently without direct human intervention. This autonomy extends to various devices and systems, ranging from computing resources and communication networks, to vehicles and robots.
- Multimodality: (Everything Multimodal) Technologies in Cyberpunk 2077 often embrace multimodality, capturing and processing various types of sensory data simultaneously. This multimodal approach enables more comprehensive and nuanced understanding and analysis of complex situations.
- Personalization: (Everything Personalized) A strong emphasis is placed on the personalization of the games world, with technologies adapting to individual users locations, needs, and preferences. This personalization allows for more tailored and effective solutions to various challenges and enhances overall user experiences, allowing people to effectively experience the world in a truly unique manner.
- Bio-Integration: (Everything Bio-Integrated) The games vision of the future also includes the convergence of the physical, artificial, and biological worlds. Technologies are seamlessly integrated into human bodies and natural environments, creating new hybrid systems that combine the strengths of each domain.
- Digital Security: (Everything Secure) The world of Cyberpunk 2077 shows unwavering dedication to securing all digital interactions, from personal communication to vital infrastructure. In this hyper-connected society, where everything is linked digitally, constant readiness against cyber threats is essential. The narrative highlights the need for robust safeguards and vigilance against potential breaches, in a way that intertwines with human abilities.
The main technologies of the game have been grouped in six different thematic groups: Human Augmentation, Brain Computer Interfaces, Digital Representation and Information Access, Smart Environments and Personalization, Autonomous Vehicles and Transportations and Self-aware AI and Assistants.

### 4.2. Human augmentation and cybernetic enhancements
Cyberpunk 2077 envisions a future where humans are augmented through biotechnology, cybernetic implants, and genetic modifications. However, in this envisioned society, not all individuals opt for or can afford these enhancements, revealing disparities and philosophical tensions among its denizens. In Cyberpunk 2077, human augmentation is not just a technological backdrop but a central element of the narrative. Characters like V, the protagonist, and Johnny Silverhand, a digital ghost of a historical character, exemplify the profound personal and societal impacts of cybernetic enhancements. For instance, Vs journey through the game is deeply intertwined with their reliance on cyberware, which raises questions about identity, humanity, and the ethical boundaries of human enhancement. These narrative elements provide a nuanced view of the potential and pitfalls of such technologies, setting Cyberpunk 2077 apart from other generic portrayals of cybernetic augmentation in the cyberpunk genre. This theme includes technologies such as cybernetic prosthetics, artificial biological augmentations, and genetic enhancements that expand human capabilities beyond their natural limits. Fig. 2 shows examples of human augmentation technologies as depicted in Cyberpunk 2077, underscoring the games portrayal of cybernetic enhancements as status symbols highlights how technological advancements can reinforce social hierarchies, and portraying human augmentation as both a tool for empowerment and a marker of socio-economic divides.
Cybernetic Prosthetics replace or enhance body parts with advanced mechanical or electronic components, providing users with improved strength, agility, or sensory perception [78]. In Cyberpunk 20177, some examples include robotic limbs, advanced ocular implants, and neural interfaces. Today, prosthetic limbs have evolved significantly, incorporating advanced materials, sensors, and electronics to provide improved functionality. Some prosthetics can be controlled by the users muscle signals or through brain–computer interfaces, while others offer sensory feedback [78], [79]. However, these prosthetics are still limited in terms of sensory perception, dexterity, and overall performance compared to the advanced prosthetics portrayed in Cyberpunk 2077. To reach the level of cybernetic prosthetics in the game, research should focus on developing more sophisticated hardware, software, and signal processing techniques that can better interpret user intent and accurately mimic natural movements [80]. Additionally, integrating advanced sensors, materials, and power systems will be crucial for creating more realistic and high-performance prosthetics [121], [122]. Academic an industrial efforts, such as Open Bionics Hero Arm, MIT Media Labs Biomechatronics Group, and Johns Hopkins Universitys Modular Prosthetic Limb demonstrate significant advancements in bionic limbs with sensory feedback and neural integration. Recent advances in haptic feedback in robot-assisted surgery, emphasize how tactile interfaces improve precision and patient outcomes [94]. As the trust users place in their cybernetic enhancements hinges on the guarantee that they are secure from external manipulations, future research should also prioritize establishing robust security protocols and standards.

A representation of human augmentation technologies in Cyberpunk 2077 helps showcasing their role as status symbols and the potential socio-ethical implications of uneven access.
Artificial Biological Augmentations involve the incorporation of synthetic or lab-grown biological components into the human body, such as replacement organs, synthetic muscles, or artificial blood. These augmentations can improve health, vitality, or physical performance. In the present time, advances in regenerative medicine and tissue engineering have led to the development of lab-grown organs and tissues, such as skin, blood vessels, and cartilage, with industrial and academic initiatives such as Organovos bioprinting [47], Wake Forest Institutes regenerative organ research, and the University of Minnesotas 3D-printed heart models, which showcase cutting-edge work in artificial biological augmentations. Currently, the ethical implications of using human augmentation are still carefully considered, ensuring they are used for genuine therapeutic purposes and not merely for enhancing ones natural abilities beyond ethical boundaries [81]. However, these technologies are still in the early stages of development, and widespread clinical application remains limited. To achieve the level of artificial biological augmentations seen in Cyberpunk 2077, researchers need to continue exploring methods for generating functional, lab-grown tissues and organs that can seamlessly integrate with the human body. This includes the development of advanced biomaterials, cell sources, and fabrication techniques, as well as overcoming challenges related to vascularization, innervation, and immune compatibility [81], [82].
Genetic Modifications in Cyberpunk 2077 entail the alteration of an individuals genetic code to enhance physical or mental traits, such as increased intelligence, physical strength, or resistance to diseases. To date, genome editing technologies such as CRISPR/Cas9 have facilitated gene modification with remarkable precision, thereby contributing to significant advancements in both gene therapy and the creation of genetically modified organisms [83], with private initiatives such as Intellia Therapeutics highlight the efforts. Despite these advancements, a comprehensive understanding of the genetic influences on human traits and diseases remains elusive [123]. Recent scholarly reviews indicate that the complexities of genetic impact on specific attributes, such as sports performance, have yet to be fully elucidated, suggesting that similar limitations may apply to the enhancement of other human traits [84], [85]. To achieve the level of genetic modifications depicted in the game, researchers must continue to refine genome editing techniques to increase their accuracy, efficiency, and safety. This includes developing new gene editing tools, delivery methods, and strategies for minimizing off-target effects. Additionally, addressing ethical, legal, and societal implications of human enhancement through genetic modifications will be crucial for their responsible implementation.

Cyberpunk 2077 presents a future where digital information is seamlessly integrated into the physical world through standardized protocols and real-time authentication based on biometrics. Amidst these advancements, unlawful access to information emerges as the most prevalent act of theft, highlighting the perpetual game between security and intrusion. This theme includes the development of digital twins, advanced biometric access systems, and improved information transfer and security. In Cyberpunk 2077, digital representation and information access are vividly portrayed through the concept of the Net, a vast cyberspace that exists entirely in the digital realm. This virtual world is inhabited by AI constructs and accessed by netrunners—hackers who can jack into cyberspace to gather information or manipulate digital systems. The mission I Walk the Line showcases this aspect of the game, as V ventures into cyberspace to confront the rogue AI, Brigitte. Digital Twins are envisioned in the world of Cyberpunk 2077 in a way where virtual replicas of physical objects or systems are commonplace, and show ubiquitous, and instantaneous capabilities. They allow for enhanced simulation, monitoring, and optimization of real-world assets. Digital twin technology is already being used in various industries, such as manufacturing, infrastructure, and aerospace, for performance monitoring, predictive maintenance, and decision-making support [97]. Siemens and IBMs manufacturing twins, GE Digitals energy sector applications, and numerous European initiatives covered by the Horizon Europe projects showcase the practical use of digital twin technologies. However, the level of integration and sophistication depicted in Cyberpunk 2077 is still beyond the capabilities of current technology. To achieve this level of digital twin integration, research should focus on developing standardized protocols for data exchange, improving real-time data processing, and enhancing the scalability and interoperability of digital twin systems. This includes the development of advanced machine learning and AI techniques for data analysis, optimization, and prediction. In addition, research is needed into integrating biometric and biological data into human digital twins that can populate the virtual replicas of the physical world [98].

## 5. Discussion
Our analysis of Cyberpunk 2077s vision of the future reveals a range of themes and technologies with the potential to significantly impact society, the economy, and culture. While some technologies portrayed in the game, like AI and brain–computer interfaces, are rapidly evolving, others remain speculative, highlighting the dual nature of technological progress—its tangible advancements and its aspirational horizons. Unlike traditional foresight methods, Cyberpunk 2077 serves as an active, participatory foresight tool where players engage directly with speculative futures, experiencing the socio-ethical implications of advanced technologies firsthand. This aligns with Scenario Planning and Design Fiction approaches by creating immersive, real-time scenarios that allow players to explore complex technological futures in ways that extend beyond conventional narrative formats [22], [23]. This interactive approach can be adapted (e.g.) in foresight workshops, allowing participants to engage directly with speculative futures, enhancing their ability to critically assess the socio-ethical implications of emerging technologies.
One notable implication of the widespread adoption of human augmentation technologies is the potential for exacerbating existing social inequalities [132]. Table 1 showed how current research directions should focus on improving hardware, software and sensor technologies. Access to these enhancements may be limited by factors such as cost, availability, and social acceptance, potentially leading to a divide between augmented and non-augmented individuals. This divide may manifest in terms of employment opportunities, social mobility, and even basic human rights. Cyberpunk 2077 vividly portrays a future where advanced technologies are unevenly distributed, exacerbating existing social inequalities. The games depiction of Night City, with its stark contrast between the affluent, technologically augmented elite and the impoverished, technology-deprived underclass, serves as a powerful critique of current and potential future socio-economic disparities. This narrative allows us to explore the implications of a society where access to life-enhancing technologies is determined by wealth and social status. Night City, as depicted in Cyberpunk 2077 (Fig. 1), serves as a visual representation of how technological progress can exacerbate socio-economic disparities. The stark contrast between its affluent, augmented elite and impoverished underclass highlights the risks of unequal technological access.
For instance, the games portrayal of cybernetic enhancements as status symbols highlights how technological advancements can reinforce social hierarchies. This scenario suggests the importance of developing policies and frameworks to ensure equitable access to emerging technologies, mitigating the risk of deepening social inequalities. Additionally, the dominance of mega-corporations in controlling technological access and innovation, as depicted in the game, emphasizes an possible emerging need for regulatory frameworks to ensure equitable distribution and control of emerging technologies.
While themes of AI ethics, cybernetic augmentation, and dystopian futures have been widely explored in science fiction, Cyberpunk 2077s approach differs by embedding these concerns into a complex socio-economic framework rather than presenting them as isolated moral dilemmas. Unlike Black Mirror, which often portrays AI and cybernetic risks as extreme, episodic cautionary tales, Cyberpunk 2077 situates these technologies within ongoing power struggles, highlighting their long-term systemic consequences. Similarly, while The Matrix envisions AI as an external existential threat, Cyberpunk 2077 reflects contemporary concerns by depicting AI as an integrated but exploitative force within human society. Compared to Deus Ex, which focuses on augmentation as a personal ethical choice, Cyberpunk 2077 illustrates augmentation as an economic necessity shaped by corporate interests, reinforcing existing inequalities rather than simply offering individual empowerment. These distinctions emphasize the importance of analyzing technological advancements not only as ethical dilemmas but also as broader structural forces that shape social and economic realities.
The themes explored in Cyberpunk 2077 offer valuable insights for practical applications across multiple fields. In education, technologies like brain–computer interfaces and immersive simulations could revolutionize learning through personalized and adaptive environments, enabling real-time feedback and experiential training. For policymakers, the game underscores the need for equitable access to emerging technologies, highlighting the importance of proactive regulations to address disparities, privacy concerns, and ethical dilemmas surrounding AI and human augmentation. Similarly, technology developers can draw from the interconnected ecosystems depicted in the game to prioritize scalable, secure, and inclusive innovations, fostering trust and minimizing risks in increasingly autonomous systems. By addressing these challenges, the lessons from Cyberpunk 2077 can help shape more equitable and sustainable technological futures.
The rise of brain–computer interfaces and immersive simulated realities introduces new considerations for mental health [133]. Prolonged immersion might blur the lines between real and virtual experiences, leading to cognitive overload, addiction, or challenges in distinguishing reality from the virtual. This suggests potential cognitive and psychological effects of immersive technologies, which imply a need of further research into their long-term impact on human perception and mental health. The description of technologies suggest heavy dependences on our ability to generate realistic interactions. If successful, educational systems and cognitive development patterns might also evolve in unforeseen ways as instant information access becomes commonplace.
As AI approaches higher levels of self-awareness and personalized interaction, ethical considerations about AI consciousness emerge [134]. These self-aware AIs could be treated as mere tools or as entities deserving of rights and recognition. Furthermore, the challenge of AI alignment – ensuring that the AIs goals and behaviors align with human intentions and values – becomes increasingly crucial [135]. As AI systems become more complex and autonomous, there is a rising urgency to ensure they act in ways beneficial to humanity and avoid unintended consequences. Research directions suggest that ethical aspects, and the interpretation of subtle human emotions will have the biggest impacts in the successful development of the technology. These choices have profound implications for AI-human relationships, leading to shifts in societal norms and the potential for new dynamics in how humans relate to technology. The games narrative raises critical questions about the nature of consciousness and the responsibilities of creators towards their creations. By exploring these themes, we can derive original insights into the potential legal and ethical frameworks required to address the rights and welfare of self-aware AI, ensuring that their integration into society is handled with due consideration of their autonomy and moral status.
Cyberpunk 2077 challenges the notion of Technological Determinism by presenting a fragmented future where technological adoption is uneven and often detrimental, reflecting Critical Future Studies emphasis on the ethical and power dynamics inherent in technological progress [28], [74]. The games portrayal of uneven technological uptake critiques the idea that technology inherently leads to societal improvement, underscoring the need for a more nuanced understanding of how innovations impact different communities. The frequent technological malfunctions shown in the game, reflect on our current experiences with emerging technologies. The frequently shown glitches in cyberware or failed AI, highlight the unpredictable and often detrimental impacts of over-reliance on technology, reinforcing the argument that technological progress is not inherently linear or beneficial.
The increasing interconnectivity and autonomy of technologies also raise concerns about privacy, security, and control. As devices and systems become more capable of sharing and processing data, the potential for misuse or abuse of this information grows [136]. The pervasive influence of mega-corporations in Night City reflects the games critical stance on corporate control over technology. Through storylines involving corporations like Arasaka and Militech, Cyberpunk 2077 critiques the monopolization of technological advancements and the socio-political ramifications of such power. These narratives serve to contextualize the games technological themes within a framework of corporate ethics and social justice, making it a valuable contribution to discussions on the future of technology and society. Ensuring robust security and privacy protections will be crucial in maintaining trust and minimizing the risks associated with these advancements. As suggested in Table 1, the development of advanced inter-device communication, secure protocols and semantic communication will impact the trust that we will place in these systems. Cyberpunk 2077 illustrates the potential for enhanced communication and efficiency but also exposes the risks of cyber-attacks and data breaches in such an interconnected environment. Our analysis extends this depiction to propose that the increasing interconnectivity of future technologies necessitates robust cybersecurity measures and a rethinking of privacy norms. The games scenarios involving hacking and data theft emphasize the importance of developing resilient security protocols and ethical guidelines for data management. By drawing from these narratives, we can contribute to the ongoing discourse on cybersecurity and privacy in the digital age, providing novel insights into the challenges and solutions for safeguarding interconnected systems.
Environmental concerns also arise, particularly when contemplating the sustainability of producing and maintaining these high-tech devices [137], [138]. All research directions suggest the need for multiple devices, resulting in an ultradensification of the technology and communications. Sustainable practices in both production and disposal phases of augmentation technologies become paramount to mitigate environmental impacts.
Cyberpunk 2077s exploration of human-machine integration aligns with Posthumanism and Cyborg Theory, particularly in its portrayal of characters who embody the merging of organic and artificial elements. This directly relates to the work of Haraway and Hayles, illustrating the lived experience of posthumanism in everyday life and challenging existing paradigms of human identity and autonomy [75]. The games complex portrayal of cyborgs as both empowered and marginalized reflects the broader societal debates around human enhancement. Th game shows characters, who are almost entirely mechanical, blurring the boundaries between human and machine, challenging traditional notions of identity and agency in a posthuman society.
The focus on personalization and bio-integration in Cyberpunk 2077s world highlights the need for ethical considerations in the development and application of new technologies [139]. Throughout the game, players encounter various scenarios that force them to confront the ethical and social implications of emerging technologies. For example, the side quest involving the kidnapping of children for illegal brainwashing experiments highlights the dark side of technological misuse. Such missions underscore the games commitment to addressing techno-moral themes in a manner that is deeply integrated into its storytelling and gameplay mechanics. Balancing the benefits of customization and individualized solutions with concerns about surveillance, manipulation, and loss of autonomy will be an ongoing challenge [140]. Our future ability to develop advanced biometric authentication with standardized protocols will impact our trust in the technology.
Cyberpunk 2077 challenges the notion of Technological Determinism by presenting a fragmented future where technological adoption is uneven and often detrimental, reflecting Critical Future Studies emphasis on the ethical and power dynamics inherent in technological progress [28], [74]. The games portrayal of uneven technological uptake critiques the idea that technology inherently leads to societal improvement, underscoring the need for a more nuanced understanding of how innovations impact different communities. ## 6. Conclusion
In this paper, we have explored the themes and technologies presented in the video game Cyberpunk 2077 as a lens to envision potential future technological advancements. Our thematic analysis, grounded in the juxtaposition presented in Table 1, and developed in the following sections, illustrated the value of science fiction and video games as tools for stimulating imagination and fostering critical discussion about the direction and implications of emerging technologies.
While the games portrayal of the future is undoubtedly speculative and stylized, it offers valuable insights into the opportunities and challenges associated with various technological advancements. By examining these themes and technologies, we can better anticipate the potential impact of new innovations on society, the economy, and culture and inform the development of technologies that are more inclusive, ethical, and adaptable to a diverse range of needs and preferences.
Ultimately, the study of science fiction and video games like Cyberpunk 2077 can serve as a catalyst for interdisciplinary dialogue, collaboration, and exploration, helping to shape a more informed and responsible approach to the development and application of future technologies.
